Hardware-to-Packet Security Mindset

Grounded in a rigorous STEM foundation and advancing through my senior year in Computer Engineering at De La Salle University, my engineering approach bridges analytical mathematical modeling with hands-on, bare-metal register programming.

Whether configuring deterministic interrupt service routines on 8-bit and 32-bit microcontrollers, simulating analog filter stability, or dissecting anomalous TCP/IP traffic in Wireshark, I focus on building resilient systems from the silicon layer up to the network edge. My goal is to secure critical hardware-software interfaces and modern network infrastructures against emerging vulnerabilities.
